# LUDIA NAIL v1.7 — iPhone Schedule

- Rebuilt Booking around a staff-by-time daily schedule inspired by proven salon scheduler workflows.
- iOS-like date strip, Today shortcut, compact staff filter and neutral calendar styling.
- Staff columns with sticky time axis and local horizontal scrolling on mobile.
- Empty schedule cells are tappable and open a working quick-booking bottom sheet.
- Quick booking creates a local appointment immediately with customer, service, time, staff and duration.
- Appointment blocks scale to estimated duration and open the existing customer/appointment detail sheet.
- Completed / in-progress / waiting states are visually distinct without heavy dashboard colors.
- Current-time indicator appears when within salon hours.
- Existing Today, Customers, More, CRM direction and ART STUDIO are preserved.
- JavaScript syntax and duplicate-ID checks passed; mobile interaction QA passed.
